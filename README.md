# Privacy Policy for GigTracker

Last updated: September 2026

GigTracker ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed by GigTracker.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage
GigTracker is designed primarily as an offline-first utility application. 
- **Shift, Income, Expense, and Vehicle Data:** All data related to your work shifts, earnings, expenses, and vehicle maintenance intervals are stored locally on your device via an encrypted/secure local database (Room Database). We do not transmit, collect, or store your personal financial or shift data on external servers.
- **Reports:** When exporting CSV or PDF reports, files are generated locally on your device storage/cache.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements
Our application uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- **Google AdMob:** We display advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers, cookies, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads according to Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google Play Billing:** In-app subscriptions and purchases are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not store or have access to your credit card or payment credentials.

## 3. Permissions
GigTracker requests minimal permissions necessary for core functionalities:
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Required for displaying ads and processing in-app purchases via Google Play services.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS:** Used locally to remind you of maintenance schedules or shift updates (Android 13+).

## 4. Children’s Privacy
Our services do not address anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
